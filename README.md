One image that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026: a dated poster with six stories,
each with a short label and one flat icon, drawn to stay legible at 300 pixels across.

- Kyiv region, Ukraine: a drone hit a block of flats; a seven-year-old boy was freed after 14 hours.
- Antalya, Türkiye: COP31 ended with no deal on fossil fuels.
- Strait of Hormuz: Iran staged 14 tankers against the US blockade.
- Benghazi, Libya: a Russian corvette capsized after a drone strike.
- Indonesia: a fire at a confectionery factory; 24 bodies were carried out.
- The Hague: the ICJ ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts   # copies entry.png to out/entry.png
```

The image is drawn by `dev/make_poster.py` (Pillow) and committed as `entry.png`; the program copies it.
